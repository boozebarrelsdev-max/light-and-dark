IN THE WORKSHOP

How an engraving
is made

01

Proof

You send the words. We set the type on the piece and email a proof the same day.

02

Setup

The piece is clamped and squared. Curved surfaces are shimmed so depth stays even.

03

Cut

Rotary tool, hand-guided. Around forty minutes for a barrel head.

04

Finish

Dust brushed out, edges oiled, filled and leak-tested, then boxed.
